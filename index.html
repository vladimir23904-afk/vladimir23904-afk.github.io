Educational Physics & Climate Science

Atmospheric physics, climate archives, and planetary science.

An educational application combining analytical radiative balance models, a 30-year climate dataset across 15,000 cities, comparative planetology, and hands-on meteorological experiments. Free and without registration; the developer does not collect personal data.

Core Modules

Structured into rigorous thematic sections based on peer-reviewed climate data and fundamental physics.

01

Energy Balance & Atmospheric Thermodynamics

Interactive simulations of planetary radiative equilibrium, greenhouse forcing, adiabatic lapse rates, atmospheric moisture, and cloud nucleation microphysics.

02

Global Climate Dataset (15,000 Cities)

Three decades of NASA POWER and ERA5 archives. Seasonal climographs, diurnal cycles, Conrad continentality metrics, and automated climate twin matching.

03

Comparative Planetary Science

Study the atmospheres of Venus, Mars, Titan, Triton, and Pluto. Compare atmospheric mass, runaway greenhouse thresholds, and surface radiation regimes.

04

Observational Field Laboratory

Practical real-world measurements: barometric floor height determination, psychrometric dew point evaluation, cloud chamber adiabatic cooling, and shadow geometry.

05

Privacy & Local Compute

No registration or user accounts; the developer does not collect personal data. Lessons, map, and calculations work offline — internet access is used solely to display non-personalized ads (Google AdMob).
