Why Troposa?

An educational platform built to bridge the gap between abstract climate science and real-world observations.

⚡

Atmospheric Physics

Interactive models: planetary energy budget, greenhouse effect, adiabatic lapse rate, convection, and cloud nucleation.

🗺

15,000 Global Cities

30 years of NASA POWER & ERA5 data. Climographs, weekly rhythms, climate twins, and warming signal analysis.

🪐

Comparative Planetology

Study extreme atmospheres: Venus, Mars, Titan, Triton, and Pluto. Compare air densities, runaway greenhouse effects, and surface temperatures.

🧪

Hands-On Experiments

Pocket barometer to measure floor heights, cloud in a bottle, psychrometer for humidity, and sun-shadow latitude calculation.

🔒

Privacy by Design

No accounts, no sign-ins, and no personal data collection. Works offline with all calculations running locally on your device.
